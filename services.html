Conditions treated & procedures offered

An overview of the orthopaedic conditions managed and the surgical and non-surgical procedures available at the clinic.

Conditions Treated

Orthopaedic conditions

  • Joint pain
  • Arthritis
  • Osteoporosis
  • Gout
  • Back & neck pain
  • Shoulder problems
  • Fractures & trauma
  • Ligament injuries
  • Sports injuries
  • Bone deformities

Procedures

Procedures offered

Arthroscopy

Minimally invasive joint surgery used for diagnosis and treatment of joint conditions.

Joint Replacement Surgery

Arthroplasty procedures for damaged or degenerated joints.

Spine Surgery

Surgical treatment for spinal conditions affecting the back and neck.

Fracture Fixation & Trauma Surgery

Surgical stabilisation and repair of fractures resulting from trauma.

Ilizarov External Fixation

External fixation technique used for complex fractures and bone deformities.
