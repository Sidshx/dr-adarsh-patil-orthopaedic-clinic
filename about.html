Profile

Dr. Adarsh D. Patil

MBBS, MS (Orthopaedics)

Consultant Orthopaedic / Trauma Surgeon

Dr. Adarsh D. Patil is an orthopaedic and trauma surgeon practising at Dr. Adarsh Patil's Orthopaedic Clinic in Sector 1, Sanpada, Navi Mumbai. He is registered with the Maharashtra Medical Council under registration number MMC2020053493.

His clinical work covers joint and bone conditions, spine and pain problems, fractures and trauma, ligament and sports injuries, and hand conditions. Care ranges from medication, injections, plaster and bracing through to arthroscopy, joint replacement, spine surgery, fracture fixation, Ilizarov external fixation and hand surgery. Patients from Sanpada, Vashi, Belapur and across Navi Mumbai consult at the clinic.

Consultations are held in English, Hindi or Marathi, Monday to Sunday, by appointment.
