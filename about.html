Methodology

Approach to Care

Consultations follow a structured, evidence-based process from assessment through to follow-up. This description is factual and methodological — it does not represent a guarantee of outcome.

Clinical Assessment

History-taking and physical examination to identify the affected joint, bone or spinal structure, followed by relevant imaging or investigations where indicated.

Treatment Planning

Treatment options are discussed with the patient, ranging from conservative management to surgical intervention, based on clinical findings and diagnosis.

Follow-Up

Post-treatment review is scheduled to monitor recovery and adjust care as required, in line with standard orthopaedic practice.
